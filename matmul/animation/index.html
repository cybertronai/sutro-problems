Naive 4×4 Matmul

The baseline triple loop, executed one instruction at a time under the simplified Dally model: a read from address a costs ⌈√a⌉; writes and arithmetic are free.

112 instructions 64 mul · 48 add 16 exit reads total 1,316 best known 800

Layout

Addresses are assigned by the baseline generator; the cell at linear index a sits at Manhattan distance ⌈√a⌉ from the core, so ring k holds 2k−1 cells and the whole program fits in seven rings.

A row-major1 – 16d = 1 – 4
B row-major17 – 32d = 5 – 6
C row-major33 – 48d = 6 – 7
tmp49d = 7

Where the energy goes

Two thirds of the bill is scratch traffic. Every one of the 48 accumulate steps re-reads C[i,j] at distance 6–7 and tmp at distance 7, so tmp alone (336) costs more than every read of A (200). The 800-cost record removes it by restructuring the loop as outer products and keeping the accumulator in cheap addresses.
