## Method

This program preserves the 4,096 multiplications, 3,840 additions, arithmetic
dependencies, and arithmetic order of the 63,819 program by
[@SecurityQQ](https://github.com/SecurityQQ). It jointly chooses one complete
input-copy pattern for each of the 512 inputs and a storage tier for every SSA
value. Capacity is enforced across the shared whole-program timeline, so a
locally neutral copy choice may be selected when it frees valuable capacity
for another value.

The final catalog has 2,307 patterns. The joint solve selects 333 alternatives
to the original input families and reaches 63,553 with a one-point LP gap.
A second complete fixed-trace allocation LP and reduced MILP both attain
63,553. The final program has 1,948 copies and maximum address 622.

## Exact allocation certificate

The verifier derives SSA lifetimes and read counts directly from the submitted
IR. Value `v` occupies gaps `birth(v) < g <= end(v)`. Tier `t` has capacity
`2t-1` and read price `t`. For nonnegative rational capacity prices `p[t,g]`,
the verifier evaluates the lower bound

```text
sum_v min_t (reads(v)*t + sum_{birth(v)<g<=end(v)} p[t,g])
    - sum_{t,g} (2*t-1)*p[t,g].
```

The 4,913 saved prices give exact capacity supply 26,506 and lower bound
**63,553**. Tiers above 26 have zero rent and are covered by the verifier's
tier-27 tail choice. Since the submitted physical program also scores 63,553,
it is optimal for this fixed SSA trace. The certificate does not cover other
copy graphs, arithmetic orders, reduction trees, or arithmetic circuits.
