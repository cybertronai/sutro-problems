record. All 256 matrix outputs are verified as exact integer polynomials.

## Search and scope

Following the catalytic-memory paper experiments, we resumed an unfinished
joint tree/storage search. The connection to
[Asadi–Cleve §3](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.02606v1#S3) is the attention to
retained subtree boundaries and their storage costs. The concrete improvement
uses conventional scheduling, reduction trees, allocation, and redundant-copy
removal. It does not implement the paper's finite-field catalytic pebbling
construction or establish a gain from catalytic arithmetic.

A separate reproducible follow-up tests 512 ways to borrow occupied cheap
cells and share restoration across multiple captures. Its best candidate costs
63,352, so it adds no improvement to this submission. The bounded search and
its exact verification are documented in the
[experiment report](https://github.com/jurajselep/sutro-problems/blob/24e1281287613fa9821cab38341a87bafff4cc66/matmul/experiments/catalytic_16x16/README.md#follow-up-sharing-a-borrowed-cell-across-multiple-captures).

The saved search had reached a numerical LP value of 63,351 but stopped during
crossover. Completing crossover returned a valid fractional solution. The
first integer repair restricted choices to its nonzero support and fixed its
unit entries; that restricted model was infeasible. The successful repair
instead allowed the union of the LP support and the known 63,358 incumbent,
fixing unit entries only where both solutions agreed. The incumbent remains
feasible in this model, and the repair found an integral 63,351 program.

The solver-reported LP optimum of 63,351 applies to the joint matching model
before copy cleanup. Its allocation certificate was not independently replayed
for this submission. The final 63,350 program has one fewer copy and lies outside
that model. These results do not prove unrestricted matrix-multiplication
optimality.
