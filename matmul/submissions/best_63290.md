## Final 63,296 → 63,290 step

The last six units came from six associative rewrites of single-consumer sums.
Those rewrites changed local value lifetimes and storage assignments while
preserving the exact arithmetic function. Each rewrite was also checked in
isolation against the 63,296 candidate and saved exactly one addition-read
unit. Together they reduce addition reads from 20,099 to 20,093, leaving
operation counts and the other read costs unchanged.

The earlier search stages are not reproduced here. This submission contains
the frozen final artifact and its verifier; the research baseline, saved
edits, and search tooling are not included. Verification does not rerun the
optimizer. This is an upper-bound improvement only; no global optimality is
claimed and the score is not a direct hardware-energy measurement.
